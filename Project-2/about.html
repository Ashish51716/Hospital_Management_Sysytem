About CarePoint Hospital

Delivering state-of-the-art medical services with compassion and clinical integrity.

From Executive Director's Desk

Prof. (Brig.) Dr. Raju Agarwal

"CarePoint is an institution of national importance, dedicated to delivering high-quality healthcare, advancing medical education, and conducting cutting-edge research."

In alignment with standard medical protocols and healthcare systems, CarePoint is fully committed to contributing towards the creation of a healthy and empowered society. We recognize that good health is the foundation of individual strength, and every citizen must have access to affordable, equitable, and compassionate healthcare.

Our infrastructure supports modern multi-specialty clinical operations, digital health registry solutions, outpatient services, and immediate emergency trauma handling.

Our Mission & Vision

The core values and goals that drive our clinical operations every day

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, compassionate, and affordable healthcare services to our community. We aim to integrate scientific research, modern technology, and clinical ethics to deliver optimal diagnostics and therapeutics.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a premier global healthcare institution, known for innovations in patient care, nursing excellence, and academic training, creating a healthier and happier world for all.

Our Core Values

We work with Compassion, Integrity, Collaboration, and Respect. Every clinical trial, surgical operation, or simple patient checkup is conducted with absolute transparency and respect for life.

Our Journey / Timeline

Tracing our evolution from a modest clinic to a multi-specialty medical hub

2012

Foundation Stone

CarePoint Hospital was founded with a dedicated team of 20 doctors and a primary outpatient care block in Patna.

2016

Trauma & Emergency Wing

Inaugurated a dedicated 24x7 trauma care facility and ICU wing to handle major emergencies, accidents, and life-critical operations.

2020

Digital Diagnostic Center

Upgraded diagnostics services with high-resolution Radiology equipment (MRI, CT Scan) and introduced online laboratory reports matching.

2025

Multi-specialty Super Block

Expanded operations to 500+ beds, 6 specialized operating theaters, a large blood bank unit, and an automated pharmacy dispatch center.
