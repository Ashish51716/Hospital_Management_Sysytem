Clinical Departments

Explore the clinical divisions and specialized diagnostics available at our hospital hubs.

General Medicine

Primary health evaluations, diagnostics, cold/flu treatment, chronic disease counseling, and vaccinations. Our specialists evaluate symptoms to provide correct medical treatment pathways.

Cardiology

Advanced diagnosis and treatments for heart conditions, including bypass surgeries, ECG monitoring, chest pain therapy, hypertension management, and arterial health recovery programs.

Neurology

Expert clinical care for central nervous system disorders. Specialized diagnosis for chronic migraines, memory loss, nervous breakdowns, stroke rehabilitation, and spine ailments.

Orthopedics

Bone and joint clinical support. We offer modern knee replacements, muscle therapy, fracture management, arthritis treatments, and recovery sessions with experienced physiotherapists.

Pediatrics

Dedicated medical care and support for newborns, toddlers, children, and teenagers. Offering pediatric vaccinations, developmental checks, flu management, and nutritional support.

Gynecology

Compassionate clinical care supporting women's reproductive health, safe prenatal checkups, maternity packages, family welfare counselling, and menopause care programs.

CarePoint

Providing high-quality healthcare medical services, advancing clinical education, and conducting cutting-edge research.
