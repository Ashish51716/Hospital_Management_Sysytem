Radiology & Diagnostics

State-of-the-art scanning infrastructure supporting precise anatomical diagnostics.

Imaging & Scan Departments

We host high-resolution scanners to resolve precise anatomical structures

MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)

Non-invasive scan mapping soft tissues, organs, ligaments, brain structures, and neural pathways. Employs strong magnets with zero radiation exposure.

Operational

CT (Computed Tomography) Scan

Cross-sectional diagnostic check for bone injuries, lung checks, tumor tracking, and vascular blockages. Rapid scans with high detail resolution.

Operational

Digital X-Ray

Instant bone scanning for fractures, dental alignments, and chest examinations. Offers minimal radiation dose with instant digital outputs.

Operational

Ultrasound (USG)

Employs sound wave reflections to check abdominal organs, monitor fetal development during pregnancy, and guide minimally invasive biopsies.

Operational

ECG (Electrocardiogram)

Evaluates cardiac electrical pathways. Rapid checkup diagnosing irregular heartbeats (arrhythmia), heart attacks, and heart strain issues.

Operational
